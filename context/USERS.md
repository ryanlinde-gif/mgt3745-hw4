## 1. Interview Synthesis

**Interview 1 — my sister, who does her own coach outreach.** I asked about her last batch of
emails; she pulled up her sent folder: August 30, six emails. Surprises: (1) The number. I assumed
athletes send large batches. She sent six and stopped, because "finding the right coach emails and
changing each email took longer than I thought it would." The bottleneck is upstream of sending.
(2) She does follow up sometimes, she just loses track of who: "I'll tell myself I need to follow
up, and then I have practice or school or something else and forget which coaches I was supposed
to email again." I assumed reluctance; it is a memory problem. (3) Her effort runs in waves tied to
school and training load, not to rejection.

**Interview 2 — my mother, who pays.** I asked about the last time she tried to work out where my
sister stands. Surprises: (1) She already spends real money on ID camps, showcases, travel, and
film, none of it a recruiting service — my competition is the camp. (2) The last thing she declined
was an ID camp, and not on price: "We weren't sure the school was interested enough in her to
justify it. I don't mind spending money if I know there's a reason for it, but I don't want to
spend hundreds of dollars every time just hoping somebody notices her." (3) She buys from fear of
omission, "you don't want to feel like you skipped something important," but that motive is
weakening; she is "definitely more careful now about what we pay for."

**Effect on HW1.** Framing C survives, sharpened. The block is not that families fail to understand
value in the abstract. It is that they cannot get evidence of interest before they spend.

## 2. Job Statements

**Parent.** When I am deciding whether to spend hundreds of dollars on another camp or showcase, I
want to know whether that program is actually interested in her, so I can spend where there is a
reason instead of hoping somebody notices her.

**Athlete.** When recruiting piles up on top of school, club, and training, I want to stay
consistent with the coaches I have already contacted, so I can tell whether any of this is working
instead of going quiet for weeks.
